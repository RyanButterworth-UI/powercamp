Power Camp 2026 — Kit List

PURITY · OBEDIENCE · WORSHIP · ENDURANCE · RIGHTEOUSNESS

Friday 31 July → Sunday 2 August 2026 · YFC Magaliesburg

Bedding

  • Sleeping bag (it gets cold at night)
  • Pillow

Clothing

  • Warm jacket / hoodie — yes, even if the day is sunny
  • Beanie and gloves for early mornings
  • Old comfortable clothes you don't mind getting muddy
  • Spare set of clothes for after activities
  • Sleepwear / pyjamas
  • Closed shoes you can run in (no slip-slops for activities)
  • Slip-ons / sandals for around camp
  • Underwear & socks for each day, plus spares
  • Swimming costume (just in case)

Toiletries

  • Towel + face cloth
  • Toothbrush + toothpaste
  • Soap / body wash
  • Shampoo + conditioner
  • Deodorant (please)
  • Sunscreen + lip balm

Daily essentials

  • Bible
  • Notebook + pen
  • Refillable water bottle
  • Torch or headlamp + spare batteries
  • Small backpack for activities

Money & medical

  • Tuck money — small amount, optional
  • All chronic medication, in its original packaging, with clear labels
  • Asthma pumps, allergy meds, EpiPens etc. given to a leader on arrival

Please leave at home: expensive jewellery, extra screens, anything you'd be heart-broken to lose, and snacks that need refrigeration. Phones are fine but we'll have power-down moments — embrace them.

Questions? Drop the camp organisers a line. See you on Friday 31 July.
